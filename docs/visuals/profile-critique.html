Hierarchy“Why this prospect, now” is two ideas wearing one label

The boxed event and the ticked list are already different things — the box carries a date, the ticks do not. That is the why now / why them split the data itself encodes. But nothing says so, so a reader has to infer why one is in a box.

Splitting the heading in two costs one line and makes the structure self-explaining. It also gives you the sentence for the demo: “why he is worth calling, and why this week.”

CutThe paragraph now repeats the bullets above it

Orthopaedic Surgery in Mattoon — newly licensed in Illinois 2 months ago. The NPI dates back 5 years, so this is an established physician relocating into Illinois — not a fresh graduate — and relocations are when financial relationships get rebuilt.

Relocations are when financial relationships get rebuilt.

Before the signals were promoted, this paragraph was the only place the case lived. Now every fact in it is printed directly above in larger type. The struck-through half is duplication; the surviving clause is the only interpretation the bullets cannot make. Keep the judgement, drop the recital.

MissingThe page informs, but never says what to do

A reviewer's strongest note was that an advisor wants the next action. Right now the phone number is a fact in a tile beside an address, given equal weight. Nothing on the page tells you to place the call, or what to open with.

Renaming Reach Out to Do this next, promoting the number, and printing the opening line turns a reference card into an instruction. The opening line is already computed — it is the same trigger the “why now” card shows.

CutA whole section to say nothing moved

Fit Across Ingests takes a full section and a chart to report a flat line at 60.9 across eight sweeps. That is real information — but it is “nothing has changed”, and it does not deserve more room than why to call.

Collapse it to one line beside How we know, and let it expand into the chart only when the fit has actually moved.

Not touched, on purpose

The identity pill stays exactly where it is. It is the thing that separates this from a list someone bought, a reviewer singled it out, and it sits above the fold — correctly.

“See the records” keeps its place too. Weak affordance for rich content, but it is the demo's best moment and moving it is a bigger call than a critique should make at this hour.
