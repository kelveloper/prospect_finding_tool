04

Build order

01 Contributions, not arithmetic Settles feedback #10 without losing the transparency the market expects. BreakdownExplorer.tsx
02 Signal-count confidence Cheapest credibility win. The data is already on every row. CandidateDetail · card
03 Trigger-first subtitle One line of copy. Makes the board read as timing, not a directory. CandidateCard · BookView
04 Saved views Needs storage. Do it after the demo unless "New this week" earns its place first. BookView · new store

What I deliberately left out. Estimated investable assets — both advisor rivals lead with it and you have no such data. Inferring a dollar figure from specialty and a deed price would be a guess wearing a number's clothes, and this product's whole credibility rests on every claim tracing to a public source. Worth raising as a data question, not a UI one.
