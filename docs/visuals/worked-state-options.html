"No answer" rather than "Couldn't reach them": the second line is the row's quietest, and it has to stay shorter than the specialty beside it.

Option D — a status column

Not recommendedThe obvious answer, and the wrong one here

A sixth column called Status is what most tools do, and it is the cleanest to read in isolation. But the book prints its headings twice per spread — that is the other open finding — so one new column adds two more headings and two more sort menus to a page that already has ten of each. It also spends width on a cell that is empty on eight rows in twelve. Worth revisiting after the headings are merged, not before.
